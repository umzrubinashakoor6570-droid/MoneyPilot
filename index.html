AI Money Coach — Coming Soon

Your money questions, answered simply.

MoneyPilot doesn't have a connected AI model yet. In the meantime, copy these ready-made prompts into your preferred AI assistant (like Claude or ChatGPT) along with your plan numbers.

"Based on a monthly income of $[income] and planned expenses of $[expenses], how much can I safely spend this week?"

"I spent $[amount] on eating out this month out of a $[budget] budget. Am I spending too much in this category?"

"My savings goal is $[goal] per month and I'm currently saving $[current]. What are some general, educational ways people close a gap like this?"

These prompts are for use with an external AI assistant of your choice. MoneyPilot does not currently send your data to any AI model.
