Money basics, without the jargon.

Tap a card for a beginner-friendly explanation.

What is a budget?
+

A budget is simply a plan for your income — deciding ahead of time roughly how much goes toward needs, wants and savings each month.

What's an emergency fund?
+

Money set aside for unexpected costs, like a medical bill or job loss, so you're not caught off guard. Many people start with a small, realistic target.

Needs vs wants
+

Needs are things you can't reasonably go without, like rent or groceries. Wants are things that are nice to have, like dining out or streaming services.

What is compound growth?
+

When money grows, it can earn returns on both the original amount and on the growth it already earned — so growth tends to speed up over time.

How does debt work?
+

Debt is borrowed money you agree to repay, usually with interest — an extra cost for borrowing — so the longer it's outstanding, the more it can cost.

How does a savings goal work?
+

A savings goal breaks a target amount into smaller monthly pieces, based on your timeline, so saving feels achievable instead of overwhelming.
